About

I'm from Los Angeles, California, and have lived here my whole life. I like to create, craft, make whatever you would call it. I make YouTube videos occasionally. I first got started when I was younger, filming videos with my iPad. I now have a Sony A6700, and it is a BEAST. I also just started to screenprint. My uncle recently brought back his print setup, so of course I started using it. Thought it would be cool to make prints for others. The main purpose of this site is to show what I'm doing. Whether I made a new video or new print, this is my space.
